# Industry-Chain Map

Use this reference when the user asks for hot sectors, industry-chain beneficiaries, upstream/downstream tickers, or theme maps.

## Mapping Procedure

1. Identify the active driver: demand shock, supply constraint, policy subsidy, export control, capex cycle, technology adoption, commodity move, or rate change.
2. Split the chain:
   - Upstream: raw materials, components, IP, equipment, land, labor, financing.
   - Midstream/enablers: manufacturing, logistics, distribution, infrastructure, cloud, grids, exchanges.
   - Downstream: end demand, platforms, retailers, applications, customers.
   - Substitutes: competing technologies, alternative suppliers, demand destruction beneficiaries.
3. For each link, list candidate tickers only after checking exposure, liquidity, and catalyst proximity.
4. Classify each ticker:
   - Core beneficiary: direct revenue/profit exposure and adequate liquidity.
   - Derivative beneficiary: indirect exposure or second-order demand.
   - Hedge/loser: margin pressure, substitution risk, policy burden.
   - Speculative: thin liquidity, high promotion, weak fundamental confirmation.

## Sector Prompts

Semiconductors:
- Upstream: EDA, IP, wafer equipment, materials, memory inputs.
- Midstream: foundries, OSAT, equipment, fabs, advanced packaging.
- Downstream: AI servers, cloud, autos, phones, industrial, defense.
- Policy links: export controls, CHIPS-style subsidies, Taiwan risk, capex cycles.

Energy:
- Upstream: exploration, services, drilling, equipment.
- Midstream: pipelines, LNG terminals, shipping, storage.
- Downstream: refiners, chemicals, utilities, industrial consumers.
- Policy links: sanctions, OPEC, permitting, carbon rules, election outcomes.

Defense:
- Upstream: rare earths, engines, chips, sensors, materials.
- Midstream: primes, shipyards, missile systems, drones, cybersecurity.
- Downstream: government budgets, allies, replacement cycles.
- Policy links: conflict risk, budget authorization, export approvals.

Health care:
- Upstream: tools, CROs, CDMOs, ingredients.
- Midstream: biotech, pharma, medtech, hospitals, insurers.
- Downstream: patients, payers, distributors, pharmacies.
- Policy links: FDA, drug pricing, reimbursement, election platforms.

Financials:
- Upstream: rates, deposit costs, credit quality, capital rules.
- Midstream: banks, brokers, exchanges, asset managers, insurers.
- Downstream: borrowers, corporates, households, market activity.
- Policy links: bank capital, yield curve, Fed path, credit cycle.
