8. What Not to Lead With

Avoid in first-screen marketing
  • Internal port numbers and startup scripts.
  • Too much RL terminology.
  • Adapter implementation details.
  • Investment research as the only use case.
  • Long architectural background before showing the product.
Emphasize instead
  • The document is the interface.
  • Every block can be clicked and changed.
  • Specialist agents work behind the document.
  • The workspace learns from user behavior.
  • Local-first, inspectable, hackable architecture.
